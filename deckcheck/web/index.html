What it checks, and what it cannot

  • A slide counts as dead when one picture covers 82% of it and fewer than 25 live characters remain. Those words are pixels and there is no repair path.
  • It cannot see a picture that ought to be a chart. It reports that you have pictures and no live charts. Whether that matters is your call.
  • A deliberate text-free cover slide will be flagged. Read the slide list, not just the score.
  • Font risk is judged against the faces that ship with Office. A font your whole team has installed still gets flagged. Embedded fonts clear it.
  • The score is a summary. The counts underneath it are the evidence, and those are the part worth arguing with.
