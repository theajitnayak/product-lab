    Why this is not another AI newsletter

    On a big launch morning the major AI newsletters overlap roughly 80 percent on the lead story. The news is a commodity. What nobody sends you is the small print that breaks your build.

    Losses lead
    Removals, sunsets, rate limit cuts and price rises sort above every new feature. A launch you miss costs you nothing. A sunset you miss wakes you at 3am.
    Filtered to your stack
    You declare what you build on. Everything else is silence. A change to a model you never call is not news, it is noise.
    Every claim carries a receipt
    Source URL, publish date, and whether it was read on the vendor's own page or is still just a claim. Nothing is stated from memory.
    Quiet weeks stay quiet
    Nothing shipped is a valid answer. No filler, no roundups, no speculation to keep a slot warm.
