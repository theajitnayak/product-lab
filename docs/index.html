The broken one looks exactly the same

That is the whole problem. Side by side on screen these two slides are identical. One of them you can click into and fix ninety seconds before you present. The other is a photograph of a slide.

Why it happens: most AI slide tools build your deck as a web page. Asked for PowerPoint, they take a picture of that page and put the picture on a slide. It exports fast and it looks perfect, right up until somebody needs to change a word.

Built and run by one person. Slides whose words are already pixels cannot be recovered by any tool, so those get retyped by hand and quoted separately.

What it sees, and what it cannot

  • A slide is dead when one picture covers 82% of it and under 25 live characters remain. Those words are pixels.
  • It cannot spot a picture that ought to be a chart. It reports pictures and no live charts. Whether that matters is your call.
  • A deliberate text-free cover gets flagged. Read the strip, not the number.
  • Font risk is judged against the faces that ship with Office. One your whole team installed still gets flagged. Embedded fonts clear it.
  • The score is a summary. The strip is the evidence, and the evidence is the part worth arguing with.
