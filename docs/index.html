DeckCheck x-ray

AI slide tools make a good draft, then export a PowerPoint where the words are pixels. Text boxes shift, fonts substitute, charts stop being charts. You usually find out in front of the client. Drop a deck in and see every slide for what it really is.

What it checks, and what it cannot

  • A slide is dead when one picture covers 82% of it and under 25 live characters remain. Those words are pixels, and there is no repair short of retyping them.
  • It cannot see a picture that ought to be a chart. It reports pictures and no live charts. Whether that matters is your call.
  • A deliberate text-free cover slide gets flagged. Read the strip, not just the number.
  • Font risk is judged against the faces that ship with Office. A font your whole team installed still gets flagged. Embedded fonts clear it.
  • The score is a summary. The strip is the evidence, and the evidence is the part worth arguing with.
